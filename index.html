HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. Seconds, not minutes

  1. 01

    Pick the payer.

    The form adapts — Medicare needs an MBI, Medicaid wants a member ID, commercial wants a group number. We never show fields the payer doesn't need.

  2. 02

    Snap the face sheet — or type.

    Photograph the insurance card or face sheet and Sella auto-fills the form. Or just type. The manual path is the default; the photo is a power-user shortcut.

  3. 03

    Get a verdict, with evidence.

    Eligible, Action Needed, or Not Eligible — with the supporting facts. Toggle between Plain English and the raw 271 response when you want to see the work.
